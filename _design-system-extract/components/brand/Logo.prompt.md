Brand lockup used in the sidebar, the top bar and the login screen.

```jsx
<Logo size="sm" subtitle="Admin" href="/admin" />
<Logo size="lg" />
```

The locked mark is **`reticle`** (the default). `shard`, `bracket` and `caret` remain in `assets/` as rejected alternates — do not use them in new work. Wordmark is always "eZ-Match" in the display face, lowercase e, capital Z.
